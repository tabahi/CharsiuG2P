# Phoneme counts and per-language-group inventories

Generated by `scripts/create_phoneme_inventories.py` -- do not edit by hand.

Everything is counted in the gold inventory's index space (`phoneme_inventory_gold`, 270 tokens, fingerprint `9438371ed6dd`), through the same `map_phoneme` the G2P uses. Languages are BCP 47 codes. Two sources, never summed: `fleurs` is the model's actual output in the `.gs.json` files (one vote per phoneme token), `dict` is CharsiuG2P's training dictionaries (one vote per word type).
